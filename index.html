Common questions

Honest answers.

If you don't see your question here, just ask — fastest response is on WhatsApp.

What time zones do you work in?

I'm based in the Philippines (UTC+8), but I structure my hours around my client's primary time zone. Most US clients get same-day responses by their morning; UK/EU clients get reliable next-morning delivery. For urgent windows, I overlap directly.

How do you handle confidential data?

I use a password manager (1Password) for all client credentials, never store sensitive data locally, and sign an NDA before any engagement involving customer data, financials, or healthcare records. For HIPAA-relevant work, I follow client-provided protocols and don't access PHI outside their systems.

Can you start this week?

Usually yes. I cap my active clients to keep response quality high, but I'm currently accepting new engagements. If we're a fit, onboarding typically takes 3–5 business days from contract signing.

What if it's not working out?

Month-to-month engagements mean you can pause or end with 14 days' notice — no contracts trapping you. Honestly, if we're not a fit, I'd rather you find the right VA. I'll even refer you to one I trust.

Do you have a team or is it just you?

It's just me. Every task you delegate, I do personally. I'm intentionally a soloist — it keeps the quality consistent and the communication direct. If your scope outgrows me, I'll tell you and help find the right next hire.

Can you build automations for tools I'm already using?

Almost certainly yes. I work in Zapier and Make, which integrate with 6,000+ apps between them. If you're on something exotic, send me the name — I'll tell you honestly whether I can build it or whether you'd be better served by a specialist.

How quickly do you respond during the workday?

Average is 47 minutes during my working window. For Operator-tier clients, under 1 hour is guaranteed. I'm not available 24/7 — sustainable pace is part of why my clients stay for a long time.

What makes you different from other VAs?

Three things: I treat your business like a system, not a to-do list — meaning I look for patterns and build leverage. I've spent the last two years going deep on AI automation, which is where the real time-savings live now. And I write — sales pages, dispute letters, email sequences — which most VAs simply can't.

Are your credit repair services FCRA / FDCPA compliant?

Yes. All dispute letters and bureau correspondence I draft follow FCRA Section 611 and FDCPA standards. I work as back-office support for licensed credit repair firms — I don't represent consumers directly, and I never make outcome guarantees. If your firm has its own playbook or templates, I'll follow them precisely.

What kinds of copywriting do you take on?

Sales pages, landing pages, email sequences (welcome, nurture, launch, win-back), product descriptions, ad copy for Meta and Google, and SEO blog content. I don't take on poetry, fiction, or anything ghostwriting-as-thought-leadership where the voice isn't clearly yours. I write to convert — clarity over cleverness, always.
